Platform Overview

BYNDIO is a full-featured multi-vendor marketplace. Think of it as an ecosystem where different types of users come together to buy, sell, promote, and grow.

Who uses BYNDIO?

Section titled “Who uses BYNDIO?”

The platform has six main roles:

RoleWhat they do
CustomerBrowses products, places orders, writes reviews, and earns reward points. Every user starts as a customer.
SellerLists products for sale, manages their own storefront, fulfils orders, and earns revenue.
SupplierOperates in the B2B marketplace — connects with sellers and brands to supply products in bulk.
InfluencerCreates a shoppable storefront, curates product collections, and earns commissions on sales they drive.
AffiliateGenerates referral links and QR codes, promotes products across channels, and earns commissions per sale.
BrandLaunches influencer marketing campaigns, discovers creators, and tracks campaign performance.

There is also the Admin — that is you. The admin manages the entire platform through a dedicated admin panel.

How roles work

Section titled “How roles work”

Every user who signs up on BYNDIO starts as a Customer. If they want to become a Seller, Influencer, Affiliate, Supplier, or Brand, they visit the relevant “Become a…” page (for example, https://byndio.in/become/seller) and submit an application.

You, as the admin, review and approve or reject these applications from the admin panel. Once approved, the user gains access to their role-specific dashboard.

A single user can hold multiple roles. For example, someone could be both a Customer and a Seller, or a Customer and an Influencer.

The platform at a glance

Section titled “The platform at a glance”

Here is what each major section of the website looks like:

  • Public website — The storefront that everyone sees. Homepage, product listings, search, deals, blog, and category pages.
  • Customer account — A personal dashboard where logged-in users manage orders, addresses, wishlist, wallet, and messages.
  • Seller dashboard — Where sellers manage products, process orders, view analytics, and withdraw earnings.
  • B2B marketplace — A separate section where buyers post requirements and suppliers respond with quotes.
  • Influencer hub — Where influencers manage their storefront, collections, referral links, and campaign participation.
  • Affiliate dashboard — Where affiliates generate links, track clicks and conversions, and compete on the leaderboard.
  • Brand dashboard — Where brands create campaigns, discover influencers, review applicants, and track performance.
  • Admin panel — Your command centre at https://byndio.in/admin. Manage everything from here.

Key features

Section titled “Key features”
  • Product approval — Products uploaded by sellers need admin approval before going live.
  • Order lifecycle — Orders flow through stages: Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered (or Cancelled/Returned).
  • Commission system — The platform earns a commission on each sale. Rates are configurable per category.
  • Wallet system — Sellers, influencers, and affiliates have wallets where earnings accumulate. They can request withdrawals.
  • Integrated shipping — Sellers can ship orders via Shiprocket for automated tracking and AWB generation.
  • Fraud detection — The platform monitors affiliate clicks for suspicious activity and flags potential fraud.
  • Notification system — Users receive notifications for orders, messages, approvals, and more.
